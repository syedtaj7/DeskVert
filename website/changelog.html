What's new

What's new in Deskvert

Every release, newest first. Deskvert updates itself, and each update is checked before it's installed. Older versions are on GitHub.

Download for WindowsSee how it works

Version 1.16.0

  • Full-screen apps, more precisely: only windows without a title bar (games, full-screen video) count as full screen, so a maximized app keeps working with Deskvert even when the taskbar hides itself.
  • Every release tests itself: the built app runs its own conversions before a release is published.
  • Always do this: after converting, the result card offers "Always do this for HEIC files". From then on, tapping Ctrl + Shift on HEIC files converts them straight away with the same choices, no picker; the card then offers something else for them. Settings → General lists these and removes them.
  • History: the tray menu's History… lists your recent conversions, newest first, each with Open, Show in folder and Convert again (the picker opens for the same originals). It's kept on this PC only and can be cleared at any time.
  • SCC captions: TV-style closed caption files (.scc) become SRT, WebVTT, ASS, SAMI, TTML, LRC or MicroDVD subtitles, with their timing (pop-on, roll-up and paint-on captions, special and accented characters).
  • WOFF2 fonts: TTF, OTF and WOFF fonts become WOFF2, the web's smallest font format, and WOFF2 fonts become installable TTF / OTF again. Every glyph is compared with the original.
  • AutoCAD DWG drawings: with the free ODA File Converter installed, DWG drawings become PDF, SVG, PNG or DXF. Without it, Deskvert says where to get it.
  • Kindle books: AZW3 and AZW books that aren't DRM-locked become PDF, EPUB or plain text, with their pictures, without Kindle software. Books locked with Amazon's DRM get a plain message instead.

Fixes

  • Pictures to PDF after updating (issue #35): a library folder left behind by an older version made pictures to PDF fail. Setup now clears out the old program files before installing, and Deskvert always uses its own PDF writer for pictures.
  • Saving web pages (issue #33): when Edge quits without saving a page, Deskvert tries again with a fresh profile and Edge's other mode, then with Chrome if it's installed, and notes what the browser said in the log.

Version 1.15.2

  • Sound from videos whose sound ends early: taking the sound from a video no longer fails with "the new file is 20.6 s long, the original 22.4 s" when the video's sound track is shorter than its picture. The result is checked against the sound track's own length (read from MP4 and MOV files), and the card says the sound is shorter than the video.
  • Quiet in games: Ctrl + Shift no longer opens the picker or the drop circle over a full-screen game or video, where Ctrl + Shift is often part of playing.

Version 1.15.1

  • Updates always install from inside Deskvert: Check for updates now downloads and installs the new version right in the app (checked against GitHub's checksum) instead of ever sending you to the download page. A copy that runs without being installed gets installed by the update. An installed copy is also recognised from Windows' own record of it, not only from the file next to Deskvert.exe.

Version 1.15.0

New conversions

  • PDF to Word: a PDF's new DOCX option makes an editable Word document with its headings, bold and italic, lists, pictures and tables. Made by Deskvert itself, so it works without Microsoft Office, and every word is checked against the PDF. Scanned PDFs need Read text first.
  • Video to GIF: a video's new GIF option turns 3, 5 or 10 seconds (from the moment you pick) into a small looping GIF for chats and documents. Only that part of the video is decoded, so it takes a few seconds even on a slow laptop.
  • Picture to SVG: a picture's new SVG option traces logos, icons and drawings into shapes that stay sharp at any size, in colour or black and white. The drawing is drawn again and compared with the picture before it's kept.
  • Shapefile maps: .shp files (with the .dbf, .prj and .cpg beside them) become KML, GeoJSON, GPX, KMZ, CSV or Excel, with each place's details. Maps in degrees, Web Mercator or UTM are turned into longitude / latitude; other projections are refused with a plain message instead of being guessed.

From the browser

  • Point at a link: tap Ctrl + Shift with the pointer on a link to use what it points to: a link to a PDF, picture or other file fetches that file, and a link to a page gives that page's options. Selecting just a link works the same way (it used to save the link's words).
  • No more copies: tapping Ctrl + Shift again on the same page or selection reuses what was saved a moment ago instead of adding "(1)", "(2)"… to Downloads\Deskvert.

Also

  • Smaller download: the installer is about 3.5 MB smaller and the portable zip about 9 MB smaller, with the new features included.
  • Support Deskvert: a button on the About page opens Deskvert's GitHub Sponsors page.
  • Every conversion now has its own page on the website, with a format finder: https://deskvert-website.vercel.app/convert

Version 1.14.0

Tapshift is now Deskvert

Same app, new name: Deskvert converts files right on your desktop.

  • Nothing to do: updating from Tapshift keeps your settings, start-up, right-click and Send to entries, and installs over the old copy (the old program file and shortcuts are removed).
  • New website: https://deskvert-website.vercel.app
  • Files saved in the old Downloads\Tapshift folder stay there; new ones go to Downloads\Deskvert.

Version 1.13.0

More from the browser

  • Just the article: tap Ctrl + Shift on a page and pick Article as PDF: the headline, text and pictures, without menus, ads, cookie banners or comments, laid out calmly with a line saying where it came from.
  • Every picture on a page: All pictures saves each picture at the biggest size the page offers, in one folder. Icons and tiny pictures are left out; each one is opened to check it's a real picture.

New tools

  • A still from a video: any moment (the first frame, a tenth in, the middle, three quarters in) as a JPG at the video's own size, or a frames sheet of nine moments with their times. Windows' own decoder reads only the frames needed; nothing is re-encoded.
  • Trim a recording: keep only the part between two times (MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, WMA stay what they are).
  • Sign a PDF: your name in a handwriting font (or a picture of your signature) with the date, on the last page, the first or every page. A visible signature, not a certificate signature.
  • Outlook .msg e-mails to PDF, HTML, text or Markdown, and their attachments as a ZIP, without Outlook.
  • Rename many files: tidy copies in a new folder, named by date taken or a number ("2026-11-13 01.jpg", "Holiday 03.jpg"). Your originals keep their names.
  • Watch folders (Settings > General): files you put in a chosen folder are converted by themselves (PDF, JPG, PNG, WEBP, Compress, MP3, TXT), into a "Converted" folder inside it. Only new files.

Reach and comfort

  • No Microsoft Office? LibreOffice: Word, Excel and PowerPoint files are converted by the free LibreOffice when Office isn't installed, in a private profile of its own; every result is checked the same way.
  • Report a problem (tray menu, About, and a Report button on a red card): you see exactly what would be shared, with your name, folders and file names hidden, and a filled-in GitHub issue opens in your browser. Tapshift sends nothing itself.
  • The result card appears on the screen you're working on (where the mouse is), not always the main one.
  • Reduce motion (Settings > General and the tour): still pictures instead of animations, windows that simply appear. Windows' own Animation effects setting is followed too.
  • Reading a browser selection no longer adds your own clipboard a second time to Windows' clipboard history.
  • New animated cards in the gallery for all of the above.

Tapshift now converts 197 kinds of files in 171 ways.

Version 1.12.0

Straight from your browser

Edge, Chrome, Brave, Firefox, Opera: nothing to install in the browser. All of it is in the welcome tour, the

gallery and the guide.

  • Drag and convert: hold Ctrl + Shift while dragging a picture, link, table, text or file out of any app: a Drop to convert circle appears under the pointer. Let go on it and the picker opens. Ctrl + Shift can go down before the drag or during it. Outlook attachments and other "virtual files" work too.
  • Always the biggest picture: when a page offers several sizes of a picture, Tapshift takes the largest, not the small preview.
  • Selected text: select text on a page and tap Ctrl + Shift. The picker offers only what fits a selection: a clean PDF, Word, Markdown, plain text or a picture of it, each with a line saying which page it came from. Tapshift reads the selection by copying it, then puts your clipboard back exactly as it was. With nothing selected, the tap is about the page itself.
  • Web tables to Excel: select a table the same way: one sheet per table, merged cells spread out, numbers as numbers. Or the biggest table as CSV. Saved web pages (.html) offer Tables too.
  • Save the page: tap Ctrl + Shift on a browser window with nothing selected: the page as a PDF or as one long picture, made by the Edge or Chrome already on your PC (a fresh, empty profile; no cookies), or a QR code of its address.
  • QR codes: for any link (.url shortcut or dragged link). Each one is read back before it's saved, so it always scans.
  • Shared links become files: Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Dropbox and OneDrive / SharePoint share links download the file itself, with its real name.
  • Screenshots in a tap: press Win + Shift + S, then tap Ctrl + Shift anywhere: the snip (or any copied picture) opens in the picker.
  • Website icons: pick it on a logo for favicon.ico (16, 32, 48), the PNG sizes phones use, a web manifest and a page with the lines to paste into your site.
  • WEBP downloads to JPG (off by default, Settings > General): pictures you download as WEBP or AVIF get a JPG copy next to them, by themselves. The download is kept.
  • What you drop is saved in Downloads\Tapshift (never overwriting anything) and the converted file goes next to it. Files dragged from File Explorer are converted where they are, as usual.
  • Videos playing on sites like YouTube are streams: dragging one gives its thumbnail. A video file, or a link straight to one (like .mp4), works, and its sound can be taken out as before.
  • Each part can be turned off in Settings > General: Drag and convert, Browser pages, WEBP downloads to JPG.

Fixes

  • The picker now closes when you click the empty space around it, not only further away.
  • Converting many files into a folder no longer makes a "Converted" folder inside another "Converted" folder.

Tapshift now converts 196 kinds of files in 164 ways.

Version 1.11.0

New conversions (all light, nothing new to download)

  • Word and OpenDocument without Office: DOCX and ODT to plain text, Markdown or a web page, with headings, lists, tables, bold and italic. Works on PCs without Microsoft Office; with Office you get Markdown and HTML as extras.
  • Apple Pages, Numbers and Keynote: the preview Apple saves inside the file, as PDF or JPG (newer files keep the first page; older ones a full PDF). Before, these were refused.
  • PDF tables to Excel: every table found, one sheet each, numbers as numbers and values like 1.20 kept as written.
  • Remove blank pages from a PDF (the other pages stay exactly the same).
  • Pictures: crop to a shape (square, 4:5, 3:2, 16:9, 9:16, A4), a text watermark (across the middle or in the corner), and a passport photo sheet: 8 photos of 35×45 mm (or 6 of 2×2 inch) on a 4×6 inch print.
  • Databases (SQLite): every table to Excel (a sheet each) or CSV, read-only.
  • Settings files: INI, .env and .properties to JSON or YAML, every value exactly as written.
  • Calendars (.ics) from Google, Outlook or Apple: every event as a row in Excel or CSV.
  • Jupyter notebooks: to a web page with the results and charts, Markdown, or a Python script. Never run.
  • Subtitles out of sync? Shift every subtitle earlier or later by a few seconds, same format.
  • Checksum for any files or folders: a .sha256 file to check later that nothing changed.

Tapshift now converts 195 kinds of files in 160 ways.

Version 1.10.0

New

  • Folders work. Select a folder (or several, or folders and files together) and Tapshift offers everything for the files inside, sub-folders included. Each new file is saved next to its original. ZIP, 7Z and TAR.GZ keep the folder's structure and are saved beside the folder. Up to 2,000 files at a time.
  • Send to → Tapshift. In the right-click menu's Send to list, which is on Windows 11's short menu. The "Convert with Tapshift" entry now also appears on folders. Both come with the existing right-click setting.
  • Choose where new files go (Settings > General > Save new files): next to the original, in a "Converted" folder beside it, or ask each time.
  • Details for a batch. When several files are converted, the result card has a Details button: every file, failed ones first with the reason, Show for each new file, and Copy the list.
  • A shortcut that doesn't fight your keyboard. On a PC where Ctrl + Shift already switches the keyboard layout, a new install starts with Ctrl + Shift + Space instead, and the tour says why.

Behind the scenes

  • Tests now run on every pull request, including a check that would have caught the invisible card of 1.9.2.

Version 1.9.3

Fixes

  • The progress and result card shows again. In 1.9.2 the card's slide-in was cut short the moment a conversion reported its first file, which left it fully transparent: there, but invisible, for the whole conversion. It now always finishes appearing, however quickly things happen.
  • Unexpected errors inside Tapshift's windows are now written to the log (Settings > About > Log folder) instead of disappearing silently.

Version 1.9.2

The result card, rebuilt

  • Nothing cut off any more: long file names are shortened in the middle (the full name shows when you point at it) and every line of the message fits; before, the name and the "Checked against the original" line could be clipped.
  • Before and after: see what changed at a glance, e.g. HEIC 2.4 MB → WEBP 380 KB, −84 %.
  • More to do from it: Open the new file, Copy it (then paste it straight into a chat, an e-mail or a folder; pictures paste as pictures too), Show in folder, or Undo.
  • Alive: it slides in, the check mark draws itself, the progress bar glides and shimmers while it works, and a ring around the close button shows when it will go away (pointing at the card holds it). It slides away when done.

Version 1.9.1

New

  • Know when there's an update. Tapshift asks GitHub for the newest version number a minute and a half after it starts and then every six hours. When there's a new version, a card with an Update button appears (once a day at most for the same version) and the tray menu says Update to Tapshift x.y.z. Nothing about you or your files is sent. Turn it off in Settings > General > Tell me about updates (or in the tour).

Fixes

  • About page: when an update was found while the page was still sliding in, its notes covered the text below. Page items now follow the layout while they animate, so nothing overlaps.
  • Welcome tour opens at once: the first page is up in a fraction of a second (it used to wait for every page to be built and every format to be counted). Pages are now built when needed, and formats are counted in the background.
  • The live picker on the first page only steps aside when your pointer is on the circle itself, not when it's merely nearby, and picks up again shortly after you leave.

The tour

  • Every format, one shortcut, redesigned: each kind of file gets a live story of what Tapshift does with it, its numbers, and every format it takes and gives as chips (no more "+20 more" on the results). The list has a colour for each kind, and a quiet marker shows when it will move on.
  • Fast, and safe: power moves and the safety promises share one page.
  • The "Try it here" page is gone; the first page already has the real picker to play with.
