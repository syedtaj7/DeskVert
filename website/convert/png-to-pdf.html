PNG → PDF

Convert PNG to PDF on Windows

Select, tap Ctrl + Shift, pick PDF. Free, offline, and nothing is uploaded.

Download for WindowsSee how it works

PNG is a lossless picture format that keeps transparency. PDF is the document format that looks the same everywhere. Deskvert does it right on your PC, then opens the result again and checks it before saving.

How to convert PNG to PDF

  1. 1

    Select

    Select one or more PNG files in File Explorer or on the Desktop.

  2. 2

    Tap Ctrl + Shift

    Tap the two keys together. A small picker opens at your pointer.

  3. 3

    Pick PDF

    The new file is saved next to the original, lossless. The original isn't changed.

Good to know

  • Transparent areas become white.
  • Animated images keep only their first frame.

Questions

Is converting PNG to PDF with Deskvert free?

Yes. Deskvert is free and open source (AGPL-3.0), with no ads, no account and no limits.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Everything is converted on your own PC; nothing is sent anywhere.

Can I convert many files at once?

Yes. Select all the PNG files and tap Ctrl + Shift once.
