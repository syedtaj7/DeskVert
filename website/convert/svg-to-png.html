SVG → PNG

Convert SVG to PNG on Windows

Select, tap Ctrl + Shift, pick PNG. Free, offline, and nothing is uploaded.

Download for WindowsSee how it works

SVG is drawings made of shapes that stay sharp at any size. PNG is a lossless picture format that keeps transparency. Deskvert does it right on your PC, then opens the result again and checks it before saving.

How to convert SVG to PNG

  1. 1

    Select

    Select one or more SVG files in File Explorer or on the Desktop.

  2. 2

    Tap Ctrl + Shift

    Tap the two keys together. A small picker opens at your pointer.

  3. 3

    Pick PNG

    The new file is saved next to the original, lossless. The original isn't changed.

Good to know

  • SVG drawings are rendered as pixels (at least 1024 px).

Questions

Is converting SVG to PNG with Deskvert free?

Yes. Deskvert is free and open source (AGPL-3.0), with no ads, no account and no limits.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Everything is converted on your own PC; nothing is sent anywhere.

Can I convert many files at once?

Yes. Select all the SVG files and tap Ctrl + Shift once.
