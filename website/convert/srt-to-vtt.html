Convert SRT to VTT on Windows

Select, tap Ctrl + Shift, pick WebVTT. Free, offline, and nothing is uploaded.

Download for WindowsSee how it works

SRT is the common subtitle format. VTT is the subtitle format web video players use. Deskvert does it right on your PC, then opens the result again and checks it before saving.

How to convert SRT to VTT

  1. 1

    Select

    Select one or more SRT files in File Explorer or on the Desktop.

  2. 2

    Tap Ctrl + Shift

    Tap the two keys together. A small picker opens at your pointer.

  3. 3

    Pick WebVTT

    The new file is saved next to the original, web players. The original isn't changed.

Good to know

  • Saved as UTF-8; timings are kept.

Questions

Is converting SRT to VTT with Deskvert free?

Yes. Deskvert is free and open source (AGPL-3.0), with no ads, no account and no limits.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Everything is converted on your own PC; nothing is sent anywhere.

Can I convert many files at once?

Yes. Select all the SRT files and tap Ctrl + Shift once.
