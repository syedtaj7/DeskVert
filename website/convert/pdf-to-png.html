PDF → PNG

Convert PDF to PNG on Windows

Select, tap Ctrl + Shift, pick PNG. Free, offline, and nothing is uploaded.

Download for WindowsSee how it works

PDF is the document format that looks the same everywhere. PNG is a lossless picture format that keeps transparency. Deskvert does it right on your PC, then opens the result again and checks it before saving.

How to convert PDF to PNG

  1. 1

    Select

    Select one or more PDF files in File Explorer or on the Desktop.

  2. 2

    Tap Ctrl + Shift

    Tap the two keys together. A small picker opens at your pointer.

  3. 3

    Pick PNG

    The new file is saved next to the original, 1 image per page. The original isn't changed.

Good to know

  • Saved in a new folder next to the PDF, one image per page (200 dpi).

Questions

Is converting PDF to PNG with Deskvert free?

Yes. Deskvert is free and open source (AGPL-3.0), with no ads, no account and no limits.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Everything is converted on your own PC; nothing is sent anywhere.

Can I convert many files at once?

Yes. Select all the PDF files and tap Ctrl + Shift once.
